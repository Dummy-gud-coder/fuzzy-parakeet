World of Warcraft: Class Leveling, World Routes & Systems Guide

NEW WORLD TRAVEL & SHIP ROUTES

WoW: Forever introduces new nautical pathways to keep the classic exploration feel while making cross-faction and cross-continental travel faster:

  • Stormwind Harbor ↔ Auberdine: Links Night Elf and Human starting hubs directly. Allows players to group up early in Elwynn or Teldrassil without taking dangerous overland routes.
  • Menethil ↔ Southshore ↔ Auberdine: A triangular ferry route linking the Wetlands, Hillsbrad Foothills, and Darkshore. Opens up clean progression loops through mid-level zones.
  • Riverglades ↔ Steamwheedle Port: Direct connection from the newly added Riverglades zone (Eastern Kingdoms) to Tanaris (Kalimdor), providing quick access to southern leveling hubs.

NEW ZONE: THE RIVERGLADES (LEVELS 30–45)
Zone Layout & Key Hubs

Bordered by Redridge Mountains, Badlands, Burning Steppes, and Swamp of Sorrows, the Riverglades contains 150–200 new quests and a central Goblin transport hub.

  • Powderfuse Port: Coastal neutral Goblin town connecting to Steamwheedle Port via ship. Main hub for repairs, flight path, and cross-continent trade.
  • Western Uplands: Rugged foothills featuring Gnoll camps extending from Redridge and early Ogre outposts.
  • Central River Basin: Grasslands housing faction outposts and Twilight's Hammer cultist camps along the water.
  • Southern Border: Dense swamps hosting the remnants of the Brotherhood of the Horse and higher-level group objectives.
Optimal Leveling Route: Powderfuse Port Hub (30–34) → Western Gnolls & Central Basin (34–38) → Southern Brotherhood & Ogre Strongholds (38–45) → Sail to Tanaris or travel into Badlands.
LEVEL 60 ENDGAME & RAID PROGRESSION
Outdoor Zone: Mount Hyjal

Accessible via Darkwhisper Gorge in Winterspring, Mount Hyjal is a physical level 60 outdoor zone set after Archimonde's defeat. It features solo and group storylines surrounding the corruption of Nordrassil.

TALENT STRATEGY & CROSS-SPEC META

Standard WoW Classic talent trees heavily reward cross-spec investment early on. Purely focusing on a single talent tree from levels 10 to 60 often forces you past powerful tier-1 and tier-2 passive talents in secondary specs.

PROFESSIONS & THE CAMPING SYSTEM

In WoW Forever, professions feature unique personal bonuses, passive effects, and a new camping system that gives every profession distinct utilities.

Crafting & Gathering Bonuses
  • Alchemy: Features Mixology (increased duration and potency for elixirs and flasks), an upgradeable Philosopher’s Stone trinket that scales with your skill, and exclusive recipes like attack power flasks.
  • Blacksmithing: Gains the ability to craft Belt Buckles (used to add a stat-boosting slot) and an on-demand Repair All ability with a cooldown.
  • Enchanting: Grants exclusive personal Ring Enchants (providing Spell Power, Attack Power, or Stamina) and shard transmuting.
  • Engineering: Retains powerful combat utilities, gadgets, and bombs, with expanded choices for Goblin and Gnomish specializations.
  • Leatherworking: Offers specialized armor kits, increased mounted speed options, and expanded elemental/dragonscale crafting paths.
  • Mining: Grants a passive +5% increased total health bonus and introduces special Arcanite Lodes.
  • Skinning: Grants a passive +5% increased damage against Beasts and Dragonkin, alongside better acquisition of rare hides.
  • Tailoring: Grants access to powerful profession-only Embroidery for cloaks (granting Spell Power, Attack Power, or Stamina) and Cloth Scavenging to find extra cloth from humanoids.
  • Herbalism: Grants Natural Talent, providing passive resistance to all schools of magic that scales with your skill level, plus improved chances to find rare herbs like Black Lotus.
Camping System Contributions

Professions tie directly into the world-wide Camping system, enabling players to place profession-crafted structures into a campsite to grant shared buffs or utility:

  • Blacksmithing: Sharpening Wheel (Attack Power buff)
  • Tailoring: Faction Banner (Spirit buff)
  • Herbalism: Incense Candle (Intellect buff)
  • Alchemy: Alchemy Lab (Campsite workspace)
  • Leatherworking: Tanning Rack (Workspace for advanced recipes)
  • Cooking: Upgraded Campfires (Increases max campsite object capacity)
