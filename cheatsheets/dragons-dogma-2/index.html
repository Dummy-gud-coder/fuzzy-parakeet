1. Fast Travel Network

As of Title Update 3.1, fast travel is significantly enhanced with permanent Eternal Ferrystones and added Portcrystals.

  • Eternal Ferrystone: Automatically acquired after completing Seat of the Sovran (talk to Brant). Fast travel can be executed directly from the map screen by targeting any Portcrystal.
  • Fixed Portcrystals:
    • Vernworth & Bakbattahl
    • Melve
    • Checkpoint Rest Town
    • Volcanic Island Camp
    • Sacred Arbor (via Portable Portcrystal or Quest)
  • Portable Portcrystals: Up to 10 can be placed simultaneously in the open world.

4. Key Pawn Mechanics & Dragonsplague

  • New Specialization — Guardian: Enables pawns to prevent oxcart raids and defend camp perimeter. Acquired via Guardian's Tome (sold by Nameless Apothecary or quest reward for Claw Them Into Shape).
  • Dragonsplague Warning Signs:
    • Red glowing eyes
    • Disobedient pawn voice lines / head-holding animations
    • Management: Stat boosts now explicitly reflect on the status screen. Dismiss infected hired pawns or toss your main pawn into deep water to cleanse.

5. Weight & Stamina Thresholds

  • Outside-Combat Dashing: Dashing outside of combat consumes reduced stamina as of Update 3.1.
  • Encumbrance:
    • Very Light / Light: Full movement speed, optimal stamina recovery rate.
    • Heavy / Very Heavy: High stamina consumption rate, reduced dodge recovery. Utilize Pawns with the Logistician specialization or store excess materials directly to storage at oxcart stops and inns.
